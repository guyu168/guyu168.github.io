    <div class="github-project-title">Random Forest Selection of Chinese Small-Cap Stocks</div>
    <p>Can a random forest improve on buying the smallest stocks? Each month, a model trained on pre-2020 data selects 30 of the 60 smallest eligible mainland Chinese main-board stocks. From January 2020 through September 2026, the portfolio returned 27.71% annualized after costs, versus 25.94% for the 30 smallest stocks. The baseline led when both portfolios restarted in January 2025.</p>
